K1. The keyboard model is a seam with two adapters, but only its reading half is drawn

Strongboth reviewers

Files: interact/keyboard.ts (resolveClearOfKeyboard 660–903, dismissKeyboard 958–988, pure decisions 395–492), ui-tree/soft-keyboard.ts (readSoftKeyboard), verify/pixel-poll.ts, ARCHITECTURE.md §2

Problem: The window model (Android: oracle, a witness veto, back) and the in-tree model (iOS/WDA: band, a configured dismissal tap, confirming looks) are two real adapters. The choice between them is written in three places: readSoftKeyboard, plus oracle === undefined at keyboard.ts:683 and :960. A 90-line in-tree protocol is spliced into the window model's function, and that splice garbled the doc (603–606). The pure decisions afterBack/inTreeLook are byte-identical pass-throughs, and their tests never reach the callers where the real bugs were.

Solution: Choose the model once from the adapter. Each model answers three questions: is this target covered, what can be done about it, and confirm after acting. The skeleton (resolve → read → act once → delay → resolve again → confirm → refuse) is written once. The pass-throughs are inlined; recheck and dismissal stay.

Benefits: locality (each model's protocol, constants and wording live in one place); leverage (guard, post-fill dismissal and pixel poll all go through one interface); tests go through the model interface, not ternaries.
